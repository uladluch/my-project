History & Genetics

What is a Maltipoo & Why Cream?

A Maltipoo is the harmonious cross between a purebred snow-white Maltese and an aristocratic Toy Poodle. This union gifted the world with the quintessential companion pet.

100% Love at First Sight
Parent Pedigree

Maltese Dog

Passes on a flowing, silky single coat, a sweet-tempered personality, and loyal affection.

+
Parent Pedigree

Toy / Mini Poodle

Brings world-class intellect, playful springy gait, strong immune health, and rich cream hues.

The Cream Coat (Cream Maltipoo) strikes an ideal aesthetic balance between stark pure white and vibrant apricot. It resists visible staining compared to pure white, radiating a cozy shimmer reminiscent of warm vanilla cream, marshmallow, and crème brûlée.

In F1 (first-generation) puppies, genetics blend in a balanced 50/50 ratio, guaranteeing that iconic doll-like baby face, soft single-layer hair, and friendly open temperament.

Wellness & Coat Care

Caring for a Silky Cream Coat

Essential care routines to keep your cream maltipoo soft, tangle-free, and radiating health.

The 5-Minute Daily Brushing Ritual

Maltipoo hair resembles human hair: to keep it knot-free and healthy, spending just 3–5 minutes daily is all it takes.

  • Essential Tools: A stainless steel comb with rotating teeth and a soft slicker brush without scratchy ball tips.
  • Hydration Spray: Mist with a light leave-in conditioning spray before brushing — never comb dry hair.
  • Key Focus Areas: Gently comb behind the ears, armpits, and chest collar zone.
💡 Pro Tip: Introduce brushing from day one paired with treats; grooming quickly becomes an enjoyable bonding massage.

Bathing & Products for Cream Coats

Cream coats do not require aggressive bleaching chemicals like pure white coats might. The primary focus is moisture, silkiness, and cuticle protection.

  • Frequency: Bathe every 10–14 days or after outdoor adventures.
  • Shampoo: Choose sulfate-free professional shampoos designed for light and cream coats.
  • Conditioner / Mask: An essential step to seal the hair shafts and prevent friction tangles.
  • Drying: Blow-dry on gentle warm heat, line-combing in the direction of hair growth.

Clear & Bright Eyes: Tear Stain Prevention

During teething or seasonal dust, light-colored pups may experience minor tear buildup. Simple daily care preserves an immaculate teddy face.

  • Daily Wiping: Gently wipe eye corners using a cotton round with chamomile or saline lotion.
  • Filtered Drinking Water: Provide filtered or bottled water (hard mineral water can cause reddish tear oxidation).
  • Stainless Steel Bowls: Use ceramic or stainless steel bowls to avoid bacterial buildup common in plastic.

Signature «Teddy Bear» Haircut

The world-famous cut that turns cream maltipoos into living teddy bears. The muzzle is sculpted into a soft sphere, legs into pillowy cylinders, and body hair kept at a plush length.

  • Frequency: Salon appointment every 6 to 8 weeks.
  • Puppy's First Groom: Sanitary trim (paws, hygiene, eyes) at 3.5 months; full stylized cut at 5–6 months.
  • Ear Styling: Keep ears long and silky, or trim them into cute rounded bob-ears.
